MiniMax Design System

A single source of truth for the MiniMax visual language — built from the marketing site, product surfaces, and product release identity. The system runs on a deliberate duality: a stark monochrome canvas for marketing, broken open by saturated color reserved for product moments.

Flagship
Models

One typeface, four weights, ninety percent monochrome, one hundred percent identity.

Explore models Read the docs
Principle 01
Monochrome by default
The page is the canvas. Let product moments bring the color, not the chrome.
Principle 02
Pills over rectangles
Every button, tab, and badge is full-radius. Rectangles live only in data tables.
Principle 03
One typeface, four weights
DM Sans carries the brand. Emphasis is weight, never slope.

01 Brand Voice

How MiniMax sounds, not just how it looks. The brand pairs editorial confidence with quiet technicality.

Do

Lead with the product. Use short, declarative sentences. Let visuals carry the warmth.

Don't

Lean on hype adjectives ("revolutionary", "groundbreaking") or generic AI tropes.

Do

Write at grade 8–10. Use concrete nouns. Reference specific model capabilities.

Don't

Bury the action. Front-load qualifiers. Stack three clauses in one sentence.

14 Do & Don't

The rules that hold the system together.

Do

Use white as the dominant background. Let product cards provide the color, not the chrome.

Don't

Apply brand-coral or brand-magenta to navigation or general UI. They're for product identity only.

Do

Default to pill radius (9999px) for buttons, tabs, and badges. Rectangles live only in data tables.

Don't

Mix DM Sans with a second body font. The system uses one typeface in four weights.

Do

Use the brand-tinted purple glow for featured product cards and hero moments.

Don't

Use italics for emphasis. Change weight, not slope.

Do

Keep body type at 16px / 1.6. Cap lines at 72ch for prose.

Don't

Push hero displays past 80px or below 56px on desktop — the system tunes for that band.
