Web app skins

Three real, full app shells — issue tracker, payments dashboard, creative studio — each re-skinned from a 2026 trend source. Every page ships a token panel showing the 5–6 variables you change to rebrand the whole shell. The principle: skinning is a token swap, not a component rewrite.

App skin 1 · Linear-style
Linear · dark, single accent
Issue tracker shell. Inter Variable · #5e6ad2 indigo on near-black · surface ladder over shadows · single accent rule. Modeled on linear.app's dark-mode discipline.
Inter Variable · surface ladder · 5-token rebrand
App skin 2 · Stripe-style
Stripe · light, typographic
Payments dashboard shell. Source Sans 3 (Söhne substitute) · #533afd indigo on white · weight-300 display · tabular numbers · density with breathing room. Modeled on stripe.com's restraint.
Source Sans 3 · typographic anchor · 4-token rebrand
App skin 3 · Liquid Glass
Liquid Glass · disciplined 2026
Creative studio shell. Backdrop-filter blur on translucent surfaces · vivid gradient underneath · 1px white border as the highlight · GPU-aware (limited blurred surfaces). Disciplined 2026 glassmorphism, not 2021 over-use.
Inter · backdrop-filter · 3-token rebrand
